About Effi

Makeup that looks like you, on your best day.

I'm Effi, a makeup artist based in Adelaide. I work with brides, wedding parties and anyone with a moment worth dressing up for, as well as photographers and stylists on editorial shoots.

My approach is simple: skin first, then one decision that makes the look yours. It might be a sculpted cheek, a coral lip or a single graphic line. Everything else stays quiet so that one thing can speak.

Services

What we can create together.

Every booking includes a short consultation, so the look is planned around your skin, your outfit and the light you'll be in.

  1. 01

    Bridal makeup

    Long-wear makeup for the wedding day, designed to look natural in person and polished on camera.

    90 min
  2. 02

    Bridal trial

    A full run-through before the day. We test the look, adjust it, and photograph it in daylight.

    2 hrs
  3. 03

    Bridal party

    Mothers, bridesmaids and family, styled so everyone looks connected without looking the same.

    45 min each
  4. 04

    Occasion & events

    Formals, engagements, birthdays and galas. Full makeup with lashes, ready to last the night.

    60 min
  5. 05

    Editorial & shoots

    For photographers, brands and stylists. Concept development, on-set application and touch-ups.

    Half / full day
  6. 06

    Private lesson

    One-on-one, using your own kit. Learn a day look and an evening look that suit your face.

    2 hrs
